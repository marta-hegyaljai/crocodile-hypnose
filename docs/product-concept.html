MHP Hypnose
product concept

A crocodile-themed, gamified self-hypnosis app built on MHP's own videos, audio trances and visuals. It shares one account with the MHP Coaching app. It feels like play on the riverbank and calm under the water.

iOS + AndroidOne MHP accountProposal for discussion

The big idea

Everyone has an inner croc

The oldest part of the brain is often called the "crocodile brain". It reacts fast, panics easily and can't be argued with. Self-hypnosis is how you talk to it.

Real crocodiles are also masters of stillness. They can lie motionless in the water for hours, calm but fully aware. That is the state we teach.

So the story writes itself. Each user hatches a baby crocodile. Every trance, lesson and game helps it grow, and its lagoon becomes more beautiful. Their croc is a mirror of their own calm. It never gets hungry, sad or angry when they skip a day.

Tagline

Tagline from MHP

The crocodile theme gives users a character to care about and gives MHP a brand that stands out next to the serious coaching app. The theme also explains the method: stillness, focus and going below the surface.

Who it's for

Growth

Habit

Comes for sleep or stress. Opens the app at 23:00 in bed. Needs one big button and a dark screen, nothing else.

Product principles

Rules we use to decide

  • One tap to calmFrom opening the app to hearing a voice should take one tap. The home screen always shows today's recommended session with a play button.
  • Play outside, calm insideGamification lives around the sessions (map, lagoon, rewards). Inside a trance there are no points, badges or pop-ups. The app switches to a dark, quiet Night River mode.
  • Rewards never punishNo lost streaks, no sad mascot, no guilt notifications. Stress apps that create stress fail. Weekly goals replace daily streaks.
  • Our content is the heroThe game is the frame. MHP's videos, voices and visuals are the picture. Every game must teach a real self-hypnosis skill.
  • One MHP accountThe same login, profile and subscription in both apps. Signing up in either app works in the other.

What it looks like

Core loop

One daily visit, five small steps

A full loop takes 5 to 12 minutes. Steps 1, 3 and 4 are optional. A tired user can tap play and skip straight to step 2.

  1. 1Mood check

    One-tap mood check.

  2. 2Session

    Audio trance, video lesson or visual exercise, matched to goal and mood.

  3. 3Play

    A 1–3 minute mini-game that practises one skill.

  4. 4Reward

    Second mood check, points earned, croc grows, items unlock.

  5. 5Return

    A gentle reminder at the user's chosen time.

The reward moment (step 4) is the loudest moment in the app: a water-drop sound, a little splash and the croc's growth bar filling up. It always happens after the trance, never during it.

Content structure

Six zones, one journey

Each zone is a themed program of 8 to 12 stops that mix all content formats, and ends with a longer 20-minute trance. Users can start in the zone that matches their goal; the intro zone is recommended first. Zone names will come from MHP.

Formats

Four ways to meet our content

Video

Video lessons

2–5 minute portrait videos from MHP coaches. Subtitles always on by default. They explain the "why" before each new skill.

Audio

Audio trances

3, 8, 15 and 25 minute versions. Choice of background (river, rain, night jungle). Plays with the screen off and can be downloaded for offline use.

Visuals

Visuals

Fixation images, imagery cards and breathing animations. Slow and flicker-free, so they are safe for photosensitive users.

Games

Mini-games

Short games that each train one real self-hypnosis skill. Every game ends by asking the user to repeat the skill with eyes closed.

Mini-games

Games that teach the method

Six games, each 1 to 3 minutes long. They use touch, breath pacing and the motion sensor. None use timers that rush the user, and none can be lost. Game names will come from MHP.

Stillness game

Lie the phone on your chest and stay still. The croc sinks lower in the water the calmer you are.

Body relaxation
Eye-fixation game

Follow a slow firefly with your eyes only until your eyelids feel heavy.

Eye-fixation induction
Breathing game

Hold to breathe in, let go to breathe out. Ripples spread across the water. The goal is six breaths a minute.

Paced breathing
Countdown game

Count down from 10 to 1 while a log sinks deeper with every number.

Deepener
Letting-go game

Write a worry on a leaf and drag it into the river. Watch it drift out of sight.

Letting go
Anchoring game

Relive a strong, good memory. At its peak, press thumb and finger together. Practise until the feeling returns on touch.

Anchoring

Gamification

Motivating without stressing

Most gamified apps use fear of losing something. That works against relaxation. Every mechanic here rewards showing up and never punishes absence.

Accounts

One MHP account, two apps

Both apps sign in against the same identity service. A user who signs up in MHP Hypnose can open MHP Coaching and sign in with the same email and password, and the other way round.

MHP Coachingexisting app
⇄
MHP Accountone identity service: login, profile, subscription
⇄
MHP Hypnosenew app, a second client of the same service
  • Reuse the existing auth backend. Whatever MHP Coaching uses today (for example Firebase Auth, Supabase, Auth0, Cognito or a custom backend), MHP Hypnose becomes a second app on the same user pool. If it's custom, we add a standard OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect layer so both apps sign in the same way.
  • One-tap sign-in. If the Coaching app is signed in on the same phone, Hypnose offers a one-tap sign-in using a shared keychain (iOS) or account sharing (Android).
  • Shared profile, separate app data. Name, email, language and subscription are shared. Croc, points and mood history belong to Hypnose only.
  • Same social logins in both apps. If we offer Google or Facebook sign-in on iOS, Apple's rules require Sign in with Apple as well.
  • Account deletion in the app. Required by Apple and Google. We must decide whether "Delete account" removes the whole MHP account or only Hypnose data.
  • Health data under GDPR. Mood check-ins count as health data. We need explicit consent, EU hosting and a clear privacy screen.
  • Later: coach-prescribed sessions. A coach in MHP Coaching sends a specific trance, and it shows up as a glowing stop on the user's river.

Onboarding

From install to first calm in under two minutes

  1. 1
    Sign in or create account

    One screen, with one-tap sign-in if the Coaching app is on the phone.

  2. 2
    Goals

    Pick one or two goals: sleep, stress, confidence, focus, habits. This sets the first zone.

  3. 3
    Experience and timing

    New to hypnosis or experienced? Morning or evening person? This sets session length and reminder time.

  4. 4
    Quick safety check

    Three yes/no questions (for example epilepsy or a history of psychosis). The answers adapt the content shown and are never shared.

  5. 5
    Hatch the croc

    Tap the egg three times, it hatches, and the user names it. This is the emotional hook.

  6. 6
    First session

    A 3-minute stillness game plus a short trance, with a mood check before and after.

  7. 7
    Reminder opt-in

    We ask for notifications only now, after the user has felt it work.

Safety & trust

Responsible by design

Clear limits

The app supports wellbeing and is not therapy or medical treatment. We say this in onboarding and on the Me tab, in plain words.

No trances while driving

Audio trances pause when CarPlay or Android Auto connects, with a short warning.

Safe visuals

No fast spirals or flashing. Every visual is checked against the WCAG flash rules before release.

Always wake up

Every trance ends with a clear re-alerting section. Sleep trances are labelled and are only suggested in the evening.

Help is close

Crisis contacts for the user's country (in Germany, TelefonSeelsorge on 0800 111 0 111) are one tap away in the Me tab.

Private by default

Mood data stays with the user. Sharing with a coach is opt-in and can be switched off at any time.

Decisions needed

Open questions for the team

  1. What does MHP Coaching use for login today?

    This decides how we build the shared account, and how much work it is.

  2. How is it priced?

    Included in a coaching subscription, its own subscription, or free with paid zones? Should one purchase unlock both apps?

  3. Which languages at launch?

    German only, or German and English? This affects voice recordings and the app name.

  4. How much content exists today?

    A count of videos, audio and visuals we can reuse, and how fast the team can produce new pieces. The MVP needs about 25.

  5. What is the Coaching app built with?

    If it uses React Native or Flutter, we use the same so code and people can be shared.

  6. What can coaches see?

    Should a client's Hypnose activity show up for their coach, and only with consent?

  7. Who is the croc?

    Do we give the mascot a fixed name and personality, or let each user name their own? And which illustrator draws it?

  8. All in-app copy

    Tagline, names (app areas, zones, games, points, growth stages, badges), mood labels, onboarding questions, button and message text, and notification text. Marked as placeholders in the mockups until MHP provides them.
